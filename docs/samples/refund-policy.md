# Northstar Demo — Refund Policy

This is synthetic policy material for a fictional demonstration organization.

Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of their first paid subscription invoice. Renewal invoices are not refundable under this policy.

To request a refund, contact support and provide the invoice number and the email address associated with the subscription. Support reviews eligibility before a refund is approved. This document does not authorize an automatic refund.

An approved refund normally appears on the original payment method within 5–10 business days. Bank processing times may vary.

This policy does not specify refunds for annual enterprise contracts, tax adjustments, chargebacks, or payments made through third-party marketplaces. Support must request clarification or escalate those cases rather than inventing an exception.
